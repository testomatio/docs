Whether you're exploring Testomat.io for individual use, a growing QA team, or an enterprise-level organization, here's a quick summary of what each plan offers:

- Free Plan – Ideal for individuals or small teams starting with test management. Includes full core functionality, test automation support, and real-time reporting.

- Professional Plan – Designed for scaling QA processes. Unlocks project integrations, analytics, Jira support, notifications, and team management capabilities.

- Enterprise Plan – Built for large organizations with advanced needs. Offers all features, including AI agents, audit logs, SSO, self-hosting, custom billing, and priority support.

Use the feature table below to explore specific capabilities in each area like test automation, manual testing, reporting, integrations, and more.
